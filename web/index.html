Method & Research Safeguards
Spatial encoder: Bidirectional GAT
Temporal encoder: GRU + Attention
Temporal mode: Timestamp Quantile Snapshots

Research Safeguards & Scope:
• Chronological Quantile Snapshots: Cumulative snapshots represent observed retweeter diffusion progress at 25%, 50%, 75%, and 100% quantile cutoffs. They represent chronological progression, not fixed elapsed-time windows or live social-media monitoring.

• Held-Out Test Scope: Aggregate performance is evaluated on the official held-out test split (221 PolitiFact cascades, 3,826 GossipCop cascades) with zero future-event leakage.

• Root Node (Node 0): Represents the news claim/article; it has no individual retweet timestamp in UPFD.

• Propagation Driver Candidates: Structural rumor-centrality heuristics; UPFD contains no verified human patient-zero labels.

• Propagation Amplifiers: Heuristic fan-out and velocity anomaly indicators; not confirmed bot accounts or live account enforcements.
